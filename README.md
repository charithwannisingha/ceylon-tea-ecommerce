# Ceylon Tea - E-commerce Website 🍃

[cite_start]A modern web-based e-commerce platform dedicated to the online sale of authentic Ceylon Tea[cite: 48].

---

## 📌 Project Overview
[cite_start]The tea industry plays a vital role in Sri Lanka's economy, representing quality, heritage, and identity[cite: 25, 26]. [cite_start]This project aims to bridge the gap between local tea producers and global consumers by developing a modern, user-friendly digital marketplace[cite: 31]. [cite_start]The platform empowers local small and medium-scale entrepreneurs by giving them direct market access, ensuring pricing transparency, and promoting authentic Ceylon tea varieties[cite: 29, 32].

## ⚠️ Problem Statement
* [cite_start]**Lack of Dedicated Platforms:** Traditional e-commerce platforms do not focus specifically on authentic Sri Lankan tea or provide verified details about its origin and quality[cite: 36, 37].
* [cite_start]**Inefficient Supply Chains:** The involvement of multiple intermediaries reduces the direct profit margins of local producers and increases final costs for consumers[cite: 38, 41].
* [cite_start]**Market Limitations:** Small-scale tea farmers face difficulties reaching international buyers due to a lack of digital tools and marketing channels[cite: 40, 41].
* [cite_start]**Inconsistent Categorization:** Standardizing how distinct varieties like black tea, green tea, herbal infusions, and specialty blends are organized remains a challenge for buyers looking to compare products[cite: 42, 44].

## 🎯 Objectives
### Main Objective
[cite_start]To design and develop a fully functional e-commerce platform dedicated to Ceylon Tea that enables secure global purchases, reduces dependency on middle-men, and improves profitability for Sri Lankan producers[cite: 48, 50].

### Key Sub-Objectives
1. [cite_start]Design a responsive and user-friendly user interface[cite: 53].
2. [cite_start]Develop an efficient backend product management system[cite: 54].
3. [cite_start]Incorporate a transparent customer review and rating system[cite: 55].
4. [cite_start]Provide advanced multi-category search and filtering options[cite: 56].
5. [cite_start]Build a dynamic, interactive shopping cart and secure checkout system[cite: 57].
