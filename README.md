## 📌 Project Overview
[cite_start]The tea industry plays a vital role in Sri Lanka's economy, standing as a symbol of quality, heritage, and excellence[cite: 25, 26]. [cite_start]Cultivated from the misty hills to lush plantations, tea is deeply connected to the country's culture[cite: 27]. [cite_start]This project aims to bridge the gap between local tea producers and global consumers by developing a modern, user-friendly e-commerce system[cite: 31]. [cite_start]The platform empowers local producers and small-scale entrepreneurs by providing direct market access, ensuring pricing transparency, and promoting authentic products[cite: 29, 32].

## ⚠️ Problem Statement
* [cite_start]**Lack of Dedicated Platforms:** Traditional general e-commerce platforms do not focus specifically on authentic Sri Lankan tea or provide detailed information about its origin and quality[cite: 36, 37].
* [cite_start]**Inefficient Supply Chains:** The involvement of multiple intermediaries increases the final price for consumers while reducing the earnings of local producers[cite: 38].
* [cite_start]**Market Limitations:** Small-scale tea producers face significant difficulties reaching international customers due to limited access to digital tools[cite: 40, 41].
* [cite_start]**Inconsistent Categorization:** Different sellers present tea products in inconsistent ways, making it difficult for customers to compare varieties such as herbal infusions, iced teas, and specialty blends[cite: 43, 44].
